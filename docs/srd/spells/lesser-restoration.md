# Lesser Restoration

Level 2 Abjuration (Bard, Cleric, Druid, Paladin,
Ranger)
Casting Time: Bonus Action
Range: Touch
Components: V, S
Duration: Instantaneous

You touch a creature and end one condition on it:
Blinded, Deafened, Paralyzed, or Poisoned.
